Molecular biophysicist who turns simulation data into mechanism.

I build reproducible scientific workflows for analyzing biomolecular systems and translating simulation data into mechanistic insights.

I earned my Ph.D. in Biochemistry & Molecular Biophysics at UC San Diego in June 2026, in the Amaro Lab. My dissertation characterized the structural dynamics and allostery of the SARS-CoV-2 main protease (MPro). I pair molecular dynamics and enhanced sampling with bench work, including site-directed mutagenesis, protein purification, and NMR titrations, so predictions get tested.

I live in San Diego and I am open to computational chemistry roles, research positions, and collaborations.
